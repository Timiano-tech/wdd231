NP Membership

The NP Membership is designed for non-profit businesses looking for support, community connections, and access to select events without an annual fee.

  • Access to community networking events
  • Invitations to training and volunteer workshops
  • Visibility in the chamber directory
  • Support for collaborative grant and fundraising efforts

Bronze Membership

Bronze members gain a stronger chamber presence with marketing support and exclusive event access tailored for new and small businesses.

  • Quarterly promotional spotlight in member communications
  • Discounted rates for workshops and trainings
  • Access to member-only networking socials
  • Priority invitations to local community events

Silver Membership

Silver members receive expanded visibility plus deeper discounts and access to leadership development resources.

  • Featured placement on the chamber homepage
  • Reduced fees for training, seminars, and conferences
  • Invitations to strategy sessions and member roundtables
  • Access to advertising opportunities and event sponsorships

Gold Membership

Gold membership delivers premium benefits for businesses seeking maximum exposure, advertising benefits, and leadership influence.

  • Spotlight advertising on the home page and member directory
  • Exclusive sponsor opportunities for major chamber events
  • Complimentary leadership workshops and coaching sessions
  • VIP access to networking dinners and community initiatives
