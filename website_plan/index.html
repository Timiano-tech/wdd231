ReelQueue

A minimal movie watchlist that helps visitors save titles, mark them watched, and revisit details.

Site Purpose

ReelQueue gives movie fans a clean place to store titles they want to watch and keep track of what they have already finished.

Key Scenarios

  • Save a recommended movie quickly before I forget the title.
  • Mark a movie watched without removing it from the list.
  • Find a short unwatched movie for a quick evening.
  • See a movie's poster, genre, and rating before deciding.

Color Scheme

  • Marquee Black — #12101A
  • Marquee Gold — #F2B705
  • Film Strip Cream — #F5EFE0

Typography

  • Headings: Bebas Neue
  • Body text: Inter
  • Metadata: Space Mono
